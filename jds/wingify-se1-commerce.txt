Role Overview
We're looking for a Software Engineer – Backend to help build the services powering our Commerce platform. You'll design, develop, and ship backend features across our merchandising and recommendation APIs while working alongside experienced engineers who will help you grow technically.

About the Commerce Team
The Commerce (Recommendations & Merchandising) team builds the intelligence behind modern e-commerce experiences.
Our platform powers personalized recommendations, merchandising automation, intelligent search, and the data infrastructure that enables them.

Our technology stack includes Python and Go microservices, React applications, and a Google Cloud-based data platform using Airflow, dbt/Dataform, BigQuery, and ClickHouse.

As part of the team, you'll help build scalable backend services that directly impact millions of customer interactions.
