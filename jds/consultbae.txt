We are looking for a strong Backend Engineer with deep expertise in Node.js and TypeScript to build and scale high-performance systems for a product-based environment. The role involves working on core backend architecture, APIs, and scalable services.

Design and develop scalable backend systems using Node.js and TypeScript
Build and maintain RESTful APIs and microservices

Collaborate with product, frontend, and DevOps teams for end-to-end delivery
Write clean, maintainable, and testable code
Participate in system design and architecture discussions

 Ability to navigate different stages of product development – from scrappy MVPs to scalable
architectures.
