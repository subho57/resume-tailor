Inventory Intelligence Platform

It is an AI-integrated inventory intelligence platform for GCC businesses, providing demand forecasting, real-time stock alerts, and inventory health tracking. It is a product-focused platform, not cloud infrastructure.

• Core inventory tracking and real-time stock alerting

• Foundational demand forecasting logic for a pilot business

• Data modeling built to scale cleanly as forecasting depth increases post-alpha
