About the company



We're building the AI-native accounting platform for US commercial real estate, a $26 trillion asset class still run on 1990s ERPs and Excel. We start with the monthly close: AI agents extract, draft, and reconcile the books, while a deterministic engine proves every number to the cent with a full audit trail. Nobody has built this before, and early enterprise engagements are already pulling us forward.
