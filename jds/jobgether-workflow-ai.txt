Developing advanced AI functionalities such as data extraction, text-to-voice conversion, and intelligent data analysis.

4+ years of software development experience, including at least 1.5 years working on AI-related projects.

Experience integrating AI platforms and technologies such as OpenAI, Hugging Face, vector databases, and RAG systems.
