This role is centered on designing and delivering advanced AI-enabled software solutions within a proprietary Quality Management application ecosystem. You will play a key role in shaping how artificial intelligence capabilities are embedded into enterprise-grade systems that support critical business operations. Accountabilities:
Design and develop AI-powered features and integrations within a Quality Management software platform, ensuring alignment with business goals and technical requirements.
Define and implement scalable software architecture solutions, leveraging modern patterns such as microservices, event-driven systems, and serverless computing.
Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to translate business requirements into robust technical designs and AI-driven capabilities.
Ensure all solutions adhere to data privacy, security, and regulatory compliance standards across enterprise environments.

Support continuous improvement by identifying opportunities to optimize system performance, scalability, and reliability.

Strong professional experience in software engineering with a focus on designing and delivering scalable enterprise applications.
Solid understanding of AI concepts and experience integrating AI capabilities into production systems or business applications.
Hands-on experience with modern software architecture patterns, including microservices, event-driven architecture, and serverless technologies.
Experience working with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform.
Ability to translate complex business problems and metrics into technical AI and software solutions.
Strong problem-solving skills with a structured and analytical approach to system design.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with experience working across technical and non-technical teams.
Experience mentoring or guiding other engineers in software development and architecture best practices.
