One of the harder moments was a production incident where a Go service in our Guardrails backend systems got stuck in a Kubernetes CrashLoopBackOff, silently failing restarts instead of surfacing a clear error. I worked through it by digging into pod logs and status directly rather than trusting the surface-level "unhealthy" signal, which pointed me to the actual root cause underneath the restart loop. I paired with the platform team to apply the fix and restore service, then made sure the failure mode was understood so it wouldn't quietly recur.
